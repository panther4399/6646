About Neem Karoli Baba

Neem Karoli Baba, known to devotees as Maharaj-ji, was a Hindu saint and a devoted follower of Hanuman. He was born Lakshmi Narayan Sharma in Akbarpur, Uttar Pradesh, around 1900. He lived simply, moved between places across northern India, and left his body in Vrindavan in 1973.

His message was plain: love everyone, serve everyone, and remember God. Devotees recall him repeating Ram's name constantly and urging others to do the same, which is why many practise Ram Naam jaap in his memory.

Kainchi Dham draws visitors from around the world, with a large annual gathering on 15 June. His devotees include people from India and abroad, among them the spiritual teacher Ram Dass, whose book Miracle of Love introduced him to many in the West.
